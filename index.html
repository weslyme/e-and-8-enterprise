About the Company

Built on engineering.
Driven by service.

E & 8 Enterprise was founded in early 2018 in Purok 7, Barangay Ising, Carmen, Davao del Norte, to bring dependable electrical and industrial supplies to businesses in the region.

We began with electrical products and services, and grew through the trust of our first clients, San Miguel Corporation and Unifrutti Tropical Philippines. As demand for electrical, lubrication and industrial consumables grew, we expanded across Mindanao.

We believe engineering is essential to people and industry. Backed by professional managers and experienced engineers, our Marketing and Technical teams help clients identify the right products, apply them correctly and integrate them with existing systems.

We keep a complete line of hardware in stock, so you can get everything you need from one trusted supplier.
